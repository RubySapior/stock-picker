What this site is

A simulated managed portfolio for tracking a trading strategy and learning from it. No real money, no real trades. The numbers come from live Yahoo Finance prices, but every buy and sell is decided by you and recorded by hand in portfolio.json.

Where this is going

This dashboard is the first step toward a real product: a paid subscription service for other users. The tool is the product — not us trading to make money.

  • Sell the tool, not the advice. The AI ships universal signals and strategies the user operates — one-tap approval in their own Alpaca account. A tool everyone runs the same way (TradingView / freqtrade model) needs no RIA registration to launch.
  • Alpaca integration (Phase 1) — free API with paper trading, then user-authorized live execution. Every trade keeps a human approval step in the loop.
  • Public track record — a 12-month public simulation log is the pitch. Trust is the moat: boring, honest, auditable, no miracle claims.
  • RIA licensing later (Phases 2–3) — register ~6 months before launch, then flip the switch to autonomous per-subscriber execution.

Getting fresh data

  • Double-click run.bat (or run python update.py) — fetches prices + news, checks take-profits and stop-losses, regenerates dashboard.js, prints a summary.
  • Update button in the header — works when the page is served by python serve.py (localhost:8000). It runs the same update and reloads.
  • Never edit dashboard.js — it is overwritten on every update. All changes go through portfolio.json or the code.

The summary cards

  • Total Value — the whole book right now.
  • Day Change — up or down vs the previous trading day.
  • Total Return — gain or loss vs the $100,000 starting point.
  • SGOV + Cash — dry powder. SGOV (0–3 month T-bills) is where idle cash automatically parks (toggleable in the AI panel — default SGOV).
  • CAGR (ann.) — average yearly growth since the start date.
  • In the chart header: Realized P&L (money locked in from closed trades), Max Drawdown (worst peak-to-trough dip so far), Sharpe (reward per unit of risk, higher is better).

Portfolio Value History chart

  • Scroll to zoom in/out, drag to pan, double-click to reset.
  • Range buttons (MAX / 1Y / 6M / 3M / 1M) jump to a window.
  • vs SPY overlay compares the book to the S&P 500, both normalized to the same $100,000 start.

Positions table

  • Every holding: buy price, current price, value, share of the book, P&L %.
  • Take Profit — the gain level where the position auto-closes into cash (e.g. +40%).
  • Stop Loss — the loss level where it auto-closes. Leveraged ETFs (3x/2x) are stopped against their 1x index (e.g. TQQQ → QQQ) so a violent leveraged day can't falsely trigger the stop.
  • Status pills: OPEN TAKE PROFIT STOP LOSS. Click any column header to sort.

Sector Limits & leverage

Each position belongs to a sector (Tech / AI Growth, Hedge Stack, etc.) and has a leverage (1x, 2x, 3x). Effective exposure = market value × leverage, and the sector caps are enforced on that effective number — so 3x funds count three times. Bars turn amber when a sector nears its cap, red when over.

Theories scorecard

  • Theories are the testable claims the strategy is built on (e.g. “gold hedges tech drawdowns”). Each has a tier (S down to D) and a status: PENDING RIGHT WRONG PAUSED ABANDONED.
  • The scorecard shows active theories only; the Theory Archive page shows every theory ever, with search and filters.
  • Long text is shortened on the page — hover any shortened line to read the full version.

Trade events, news

  • Trade Events — the log of every closed trade (take-profit / stop-loss), including the realized P&L, plus cash-deployment and re-entry actions.
  • News — recent Yahoo headlines for your tickers, tagged with the theories they relate to and a sentiment chip (positive negative neutral) from VADER.

Market Fear Gauge

  • Eight crash scenarios (F1–F8) — AI-pop, yen-carry unwind, China shock, inflation, war, rates, credit, recession — each scored 1–5 (5 = panic).
  • The arrow shows whether the fear is rising / falling / flat; the “signals” line names the top 2 market factors driving the score.
  • Complacency is the opposite gauge: it combines market stretch (how extended QQQ is) with the fear scores into a regime label. Fragility = equities at highs while bonds/credit are stressed (keep hedges on, they may lag until the equity crack); stress = broad equity drawdown, hedges should be paying; complacency = melt-up, keep the hedge floor. A check line names the dominant fear’s expected payers and their recent returns.
  • Suggested hedge actions appears only when a fear is genuinely confirmed (high for several days). It is a recommendation for you to review — the system never trades by itself.

Metrics math (update.py)

  • Max drawdown — min over all snapshots of (value − running_peak) / running_peak, returned as a negative % (update.py:459).
  • Sharpe (annualized) — mean(daily_returns) / sample_std(daily_returns) × √252. Needs ≥3 snapshots, returns null when std is 0 (update.py:471).
  • CAGR — ((total / start_value)^(365.25 / elapsed_days) − 1) (update.py:485).
  • Benchmark — SPY 2y history normalized so its price at (or before) start_date equals start_value, then aligned to portfolio snapshot dates by carrying the last SPY close at or before each snapshot date (update.py:356). Same Sharpe / drawdown / total-return formulas run on the normalized SPY series.
  • Total return is anchored to meta.start_value (100000) / meta.start_date, not to day one of the chart.

Effective exposure & leverage

  • Per position: effective_value = current_value × leverage (leverage from meta.limits.position_exposure.<TICKER>.leverage).
  • Sector caps in meta.limits.sector_limits are checked on effective exposure, so a 3x fund consumes 3× its market value against the cap.
  • leverage_factor in dashboard.js = book-wide effective exposure ÷ market value.

Exits: take-profit, stop-loss, vol-halt

  • Take profit fires when px ≥ buy_price × (1 + take_profit_pct); proceeds go to cash, event logged with realized P&L (update.py:425).
  • Index-referenced stops (v5.1): leveraged funds carry underlying (the 1x index) + underlying_stop_pct + underlying_buy_price. The stop fires on the 1x underlying crossing underlying_buy_price × (1 + underlying_stop_pct) — vol-aware, no whipsaw from leveraged daily decay. The wrapper stop_loss_pct remains only as a wide gap-through backstop (update.py:412).
  • Every stop is tagged state: "vol_halt" and records reclaim_ticker, reclaim_level, reentry_amount for the re-entry protocol.
  • Re-entry protocol (v5.2): a stop is a pause, not a death — the linked theory is marked paused (only if no open position still tests it), proceeds park in SGOV. If the 1x underlying closes back above the stop level for 2 consecutive sessions, the theory is re-affirmed and the position re-entered with recovered capital. If no reclaim within 60 days, the theory is formally abandoned (falsified). Prevents the “silent-SGOV trap” where the book quietly becomes a T-bill portfolio (portfolio.json meta.limits.re_entry).

No idle cash policy

On every run, update.py auto-buys SGOV (0–3 month T-bills) with any cash above the CASH_BUFFER so dry powder never sits idle — unless the dry-powder toggle in the AI panel is set to Cash. SGOV + Cash on the cards is the full liquidity picture.

Fear Gauge math (fears.py)

  • Self-relative percentile ranking: every component value is percentile-ranked against its own trailing 260-trading-day window (WINDOW) — absolute levels mean nothing, only regime matters (fears.py:160).
  • Level — weighted average of 1–2 components, normalized 0..1. Components: level of a price, drawdown from 52w high, distance below 200d MA, n-day momentum, or a ratio (e.g. TIP/IEF breakeven proxy, HYG/LQD credit proxy, QQQ/RSP concentration).
  • Structural fears (F1/F4/F6/F8): score_pct = 0.7 × level + 0.3 × 50d-trend percentile.
  • Episodic fears (F2/F3/F5/F7): score_pct = 0.7 × 5d-velocity percentile + 0.3 × level.
  • Score = 1 + 4 × score_pct, rounded to 1 decimal, clamped 1–5 (fears.py:282). The top-2 contributing components become the displayed signals.
  • Confirmation: score ≥ ACTIVE_THRESHOLD (4.0) sustained for confirm_days — 3 for structural, 2 for episodic — tracked in meta.fear_state (days_above). Only confirmed fears trigger hedge sizing.
  • trend_dir — rising if score changed +0.15 or more vs yesterday, falling at −0.15, else flat.
  • Degraded — a fear whose data sources failed to fetch is flagged rather than dropped silently.

Complacency index math (fears.py:297)

  • Valuation stretch = 0.5 × (QQQ 20d momentum percentile) + 0.5 × (QQQ vs 200d MA distance percentile) — the equity side of the gauge.
  • Fear term = 1 − (mean(top-3 fear scores) − 1) / 4 — 1.0 when no fear, near 0 in a panic (the systemic side).
  • Index = stretch × fear term, 0..1 — one number for the header. But the reading is a 2D regime matrix (stretch on one axis, top-3 fear average on the other) so a low index no longer conflates two opposite realities:
    • Fragility (stretch ≥ 0.5 + fear ≥ 3.5) — SPY at/near ATH while bonds/credit/FX are stressed. Macro divergence; equities expected to crack. This is today’s reading.
    • Stress (stretch < 0.5 + fear ≥ 3.5) — broad equity drawdown active; hedges should be paying.
    • Complacency (stretch ≥ 0.5 + fear < 2.5) — melt-up; keep the baseline hedge floor.
    • Neutral (stretch < 0.5 + fear < 2.5) — quiet equilibrium; no hedge change warranted.
    • Watchful / Moderate (fear 2.5–3.5 middle band) — some stress under the surface; keep hedges on.
  • Hedge attribution check — the sub-line judges only the dominant fear’s hedge_ticks (the instruments supposed to pay in that scenario) over their last 10 sessions. In a rates shock (F6) it checks SGOV only — ZROZ/TIP are expected to bleed and are not flagged.

Hedge sizing math (fears.py:30)

  • Each fear maps to instruments and a requested size in pp of book: F1 → QFLR +2 / BTAL +2; F2 → FXY +3 / DBMF +2; F3 → GLD +2 / DBMF +2; F4 → GLD +3 / SGOV +2; F5 → GLD +2 / DBMF +2; F6 → SGOV +4; F7 → BTAL +3 / DBMF +2; F8 → ZROZ +3 / BTAL +2.
  • For confirmed active fears, per-instrument demand = the max delta across fears; duplicates keep every contributing fear id as a reason.
  • Headroom = max(0, HEDGE_CAP (45%) − current Hedge Stack effective share); scale = min(1, headroom / total demand).
  • Final suggestions are scaled demand, floored at 0.5pp; empty list means nothing worth showing (fears.py:330).
  • Recommendation-only — the dashboard displays it for review; nothing ever trades automatically.

News sentiment (news.py)

VADER compound score over each headline: ≥ +0.05 → positive, ≤ −0.05 → negative, otherwise neutral. Headlines are tagged with the theories whose tickers appear, and industry/sector labels are matched by keyword.

End goal & roadmap

  • UI v1.0 — accounts: sign-in, and every user gets a personalized AI tuned to their risk tolerance and focus.
  • Engine v1.0 — the AI makes 100% of the trades from real-time sentiment with AI analysis, rebalancing weekly across hedges / leading sectors.
  • Business staging (v1 goal = paid subscription for other users):
    • Phase 1 (now, unregistered) — universal signals + paper trading + user-authorized live execution via Alpaca (Robinhood's official API is discontinued; Alpaca is the standard). The AI stays read-only; its proposals become human-approved market orders, executed deterministically at the live price.
    • Phase 2 (~6 months before launch) — RIA registration: Form ADV (Parts 1 & 2), compliance program + CCO, code of ethics, cybersecurity, business-continuity + trade-error procedures. Registration is NOT gated on AUM — being paid for discretionary management is the trigger.
    • Phase 3 (registered) — flip the switch: AI may auto-execute per-subscriber; marketing may say the real words.
  • On-ramp routes (each legal today): sell the software / universal signals first; B2B white-label to licensed RIAs; sell the data/API feed; state RIA when revenue + track record justify it.
  • Trust is the moat — "AI trading subscription" is the scam capital of the internet; the differentiator is a public track record, paper-trading history, transparent methodology, no miracle claims.
